02 — The Atelier

We work at the
pace of the stone.

The atelier began in a Jaipur workshop with a single slab of Makrana marble and a belief that surfaces deserve the same care as the objects placed upon them. Today we partner with twelve quarries across Italy, Turkey, Spain and India — selecting blocks in person, cutting to the grain, and finishing each surface by hand.

Nothing is pulled from a warehouse shelf. Every order is a conversation between the material, the architecture and the people who will live with it.

Aarav Mehta Founder & Creative Director

04 — How We Work

From first sketch
to final slab.

A single point of contact from enquiry to installation. We work alongside architects, interior designers and private clients — reading drawings, resolving details and protecting the intent of the room.

01

Conversation

We begin with the room, the light and the way you want it to feel — not a catalogue number.

02

Curation

A shortlist of surfaces and samples, selected for tone, movement and how they will age.

03

Fabrication

Blocks are cut to grain, edges are profiled and every slab is dry-laid before it leaves the atelier.

04

Installation

Our fitters work with your site team to set, seal and finish each surface with care.

Typical lead time · 4–8 weeks from approved drawings
